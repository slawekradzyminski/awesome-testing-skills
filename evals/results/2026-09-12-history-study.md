A fresh curator selected four historical functional mechanisms without reading the skills or prior scores. Each admitted before/fix pair was reproduced twice before candidate execution. The four mechanisms are duplicate-email registration returning 500, a falsely empty cart during detail loading, mobile product-detail overflow, and a stale cart quantity after refetch. A corrected UI control and an unavailable API complete the six-task design. Documentation-only defects are excluded.

The frozen schedule contains six tasks × three conditions × two repetitions. The normal request receives original source/docs, an instance, scope, tools, budget and output location. It receives no evaluator-written testing checklist or reporting template. The other conditions add a compact template or the complete existing skill. All retain ordinary coding-agent instructions and the same browser-tool helper. The skills were not adjusted after case selection or candidate output.
