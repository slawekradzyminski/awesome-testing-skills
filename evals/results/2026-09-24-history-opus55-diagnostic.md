This is a **six-run subset of one ticket**, not the planned 36-run historical study. The [frozen historical protocol](../history-study/protocol.md) compares an ordinary request, a report template and the full UI skill. No candidate source or supplied skill copy changed.

The known-answer check passed twice on the defective bundle and twice on the fixed bundle after that correction. H5's frozen rule requires an edit in another ordinary UI tab, a controlled offline/online return that causes a real cart refetch, and a **retained original-tab editor** showing stale quantity while the refetch response and totals show the new quantity. The [fixture check](../history-study/check_legacy_ui.py) demonstrates this contrast on the defective revision and its absence on the fixed one. None of the six candidates used that two-tab/reconnect sequence. All instead found a real, simpler failure after clicking **Update** in the same tab: the server and totals advanced but the stepper showed the old quantity. Several then clicked the leftover Update button and observed it revert the server cart. Those are valid and actionable findings; they do not meet the prespecified H5 route, so H5 remains 0/2 in every condition. A separate fixed-revision runtime check showed the direct-Update path at quantity 4 with matching totals and no leftover button.

The ordinary request's first run also captured a long product name pushing its line total and Remove control out of view at 1280 px in [this screenshot](2026-09-24-history-opus55-diagnostic/run-12/evidence/cart_reload_longname.png). It is an additional runtime observation from one run, not a treatment advantage. Run times and estimated costs varied (90–132 seconds; $0.209–$0.305); six sessions on one ticket cannot support a speed or cost claim.

The full-skill reports were more structured in this subset: both had explicit passed/failed/not-run check ledgers. Each had screenshots and saved cart JSON. Neither recorded the requested correlated browser HTTP exchange log, and neither proposed unconfirmed ticket criteria in this one-shot, ticket-free setting. The unguided/template conditions also lacked HTTP logs. The skill therefore showed a small reporting-format signal here, while its stronger evidence-package and conversational-intake instructions were not reliably followed. Both full-skill candidates read `SKILL.md` but did not open its reporting reference. A separate [API/UI evidence smoke check](2026-09-24-claude-ablation.md#evidence-package-behavior-check) showed that a fresh full-skill session *can* produce those artifacts; that single success is not a reliability estimate. After inspecting this diagnostic, we moved the minimum deliverable into the top-level skills and made the one-shot criteria fallback and independent-reproduction wording more explicit. These later edits were **not** part of the frozen historical candidate snapshot and have not been measured in this table.
