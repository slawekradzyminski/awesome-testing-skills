# Report template

Adapt the amount of detail to the assessment. This template defines the output, not a testing procedure.

## Session

Scope, environment/build, available context, significant limitations and overall assessment.

## Findings

One independently actionable problem per finding:

- Stable ID, title, type, evidence state and status.
- Preconditions and minimal reproduction.
- Expected behaviour and its basis; actual result with evidence links.
- Affected users, demonstrated impact and workaround.
- Severity with an impact-based rationale; use the team's scale or High / Medium / Low / Unassessed.
- Observable acceptance criteria and retest status.

Distinguish confirmed defects from suspicions and suggestions. Related labels do not create extra bugs.

## Coverage and handoff

What was checked and passed, what remains untested or blocked, useful next work, and cleanup or owner reset still needed.
