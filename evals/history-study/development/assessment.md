# Development evidence and treatment decision

This follow-up uses the current API/UI skills without new behavioral changes. The earlier development pass already corrected repeated outage probing and incomplete missing-source handoff. The Dispatch Desk comparison then showed discovery/evidence ceilings, modest severity inflation and scope drift across conditions. It did not establish a general effectiveness benefit or justify adding another bug checklist.

We retain that skill snapshot and the same compact reporting template. This isolates the effect of changing the evaluation tasks and context quality. Simplifying the skill at the same time would make the next result harder to interpret. A short template remains a separate comparison condition and a possible simpler deliverable.

Development inputs: the original practical assessment and the complete Dispatch Desk study. Those applications and artificial seeded mechanisms are excluded from the historical functional cases. Historical case selection is assigned to a fresh subagent with no access to the skill files or prior results. This is separation from skill authoring, not an independent human research team; the coordinating author will necessarily inspect cases for infrastructure validation.

Before candidate execution, freeze the selected cases, original source/documentation revisions, runtime adapters, prompts, rubric, treatment snapshot and schedule. No skill changes based on selected historical faults or candidate outcomes. Do not selectively extend the study until a preferred winner appears.
